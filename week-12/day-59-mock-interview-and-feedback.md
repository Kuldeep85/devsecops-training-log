# Day 59 — Mock interview and feedback

**Week 12 · about 75 minutes · Run in: Video call or recording**

## Why this matters
Speaking under pressure is a separate skill from knowing the answers.

## Goal today
Do one full mock interview and collect feedback.

## Do this (run each block, then read the output before moving on)
1. Ask a friend or colleague to play the interviewer using your question lists from Days 52–53.
2. Include: your 2-minute project story, one live command demo (for example `ss -tulpn` or a Git conflict), and one scenario question.
3. Ask for feedback on clarity, confidence and gaps.
4. Write three improvements and practise them.

## What you should see
Three concrete improvements written down.

## Your exercise (type it yourself, don't copy)
1. Repeat the weakest answer three times, out loud.
2. Add the feedback to `interview/mock-feedback.md`.
3. Schedule a second mock for next week.

## Save your evidence
Commit `interview/mock-feedback.md`.
