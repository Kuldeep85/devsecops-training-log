# Day 56 — Apply: first batch to Tier 1 employers

**Week 12 · about 90 minutes · Run in: Browser + tracker workbook**

## Why this matters
Applications are a skill you improve with feedback. Start with a small, careful batch.

## Goal today
Send 5 tailored applications and log them.

## Do this (run each block, then read the output before moving on)
1. From the roadmap page, pick 5 Tier 1 employers with openings that fit (associate DevOps, release engineer, DevSecOps, Azure DevOps).
2. For each: read the job description, list its top 5 keywords, and check they appear in your CV **only where they are true**.
3. Apply on the company careers page, not through an aggregator, where possible.
4. Log each in the **Applications** tab (date, company, role, link, CV version).
5. Send a short message to a recruiter or engineer at each company (see the template in the outreach guide of the roadmap page).

## What you should see
Five entries in the tracker. Follow-up dates set for 7 days later.

## Your exercise (type it yourself, don't copy)
1. Write down one thing you would change about your CV after reading five job descriptions.
2. Note the skills that appear in all five ads and check your plan covers them.
3. Set a daily 20-minute practice reminder that continues during applications.

## Save your evidence
Update the tracker. No commit needed unless you improved your public repos.

Then commit and push (from the training repo):

```bash
cd ~/repos/devsecops-training-log
tools/finish-day.sh 56 "first application batch"
```
