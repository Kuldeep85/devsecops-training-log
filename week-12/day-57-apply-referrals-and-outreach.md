# Day 57 — Apply: referrals and outreach

**Week 12 · about 60 minutes · Run in: LinkedIn + tracker**

## Why this matters
Referrals convert far better than cold applications, and engineers respond to specific, polite messages.

## Goal today
Send 5 personalised messages and ask for one referral.

## Do this (run each block, then read the output before moving on)
Use this structure (3–4 lines):

> Hi <name>, I'm an application developer in Bangalore with 10+ years in IT, building DevSecOps skills. I built a public pipeline lab that runs secrets, SAST, dependency, container and DAST scans in Azure DevOps and GitHub Actions: <link>. I saw <role> at <company> and would value 10 minutes of your view on whether my profile is a fit. Happy to share my CV.

Rules: be specific, keep it short, never exaggerate, and thank people whether or not they reply.

## What you should see
Five messages sent; note who replied.

## Your exercise (type it yourself, don't copy)
1. Ask a former colleague whether they can refer you anywhere.
2. Join one local DevOps meetup or online community and introduce yourself.
3. Write down the answer to 'why DevSecOps?' in three honest sentences.
